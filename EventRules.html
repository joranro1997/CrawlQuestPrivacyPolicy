CrawlQuest Event Rules

Version 1 · 8 October 2026

These rules apply to CrawlQuest events: to whoever organises one, to everyone who joins and to the event's route, including its chat. You accept them before you join or organise an event for the first time. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. If you break these rules, we can remove your event or restrict your account.

1. What events are

An event is a plan for a group bar crawl, with a day, a time, a meeting point and people signed up. When the organiser starts it, it becomes a CrawlQuest route like any other, with its rules, its leaderboard and its chat. There are three types:

  • Public with approval: it appears on the "Nearby" board and the organiser accepts or declines each person.
  • Public and open: it appears on the board and anyone who signs up gets a spot straight away, if there's one left.
  • Private: it doesn't appear on the board. You can only reach it with its link or its QR code.

You can browse the board without an account. To join or organise an event you need to sign in with Apple (on iPhone and iPad), Google or Discord. Publishing on the board and using a saved route are Premium features; private events and joining are free.

How people meet

  • Always at a bar or a public place on Google Maps (a square, a station, a park…), never at an address or someone's home.
  • Only people who are going see the exact place, the route and who's going. Everyone else sees the area and an approximate distance. The exception is the open route of a private event with code entry: once it starts, anyone with the code can see it, and it begins at the meeting point.
  • A public event needs at least 3 people, including the organiser, before it can start.
  • If an event hasn't started 2 hours after its time, it's cancelled automatically.
  • There are no private messages: the only chat is the route's chat, and it opens when the event starts.
  • There are no filters by sex or age.
  • Nothing commercial: no prices, no tickets and no promoting venues.

2. Over-18s only

Events are only for people aged 18 or over, or the legal drinking age in your country if higher. The first time you join or organise an event you declare that you're 18 or over, and we don't check it in any other way; if the legal drinking age in your country is higher, only join or organise if you've reached it. If someone looks underage, report them with the reason "Involves minors".

3. Joining and leaving

  • To join you need an account with a nickname, to have accepted these rules, to have declared that you're 18 or over, for your account not to be restricted and for there to be no block between you and the organiser.
  • For an event with approval, you request a spot and the organiser decides. If they decline you, you can't request a spot at that event again.
  • You can leave until the event starts, and sign up again up to 2 times.
  • If the organiser removes you from the event, you can't come back to it with your account, not even with the link.
  • If the organiser changes the time by more than 15 minutes, the meeting point or the route, we let everyone who's going or has requested a spot know. If they can't make it, they cancel the event and we let everyone know too.

4. The event's route

  • When the event starts, everyone who's going gets a "has started" notice. Nobody enters the route unless they choose to join.
  • The route of a public event, and of a private event without code entry, is closed: only the organiser and the people going can see it and enter it, with their account. Its code isn't shown.
  • For a private event with code entry, the route is open: anyone with the code can enter, even without an account, just like a regular route.
  • On the route of a public event, your live location is off until you switch it on.
  • The route ends when the organiser closes it or, otherwise, 18 hours after it started.

5. What's not allowed

Not in the event, not in your nickname or photo, not in the route's chat and not in person:

  • Sexual content of any kind.
  • Any form of child sexual exploitation or abuse, even implied. We remove the content, restrict or delete the account and report it to the authorities.
  • Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination, hate speech and violence of any kind.
  • Drugs and any other illegal activity.
  • Pretending to be someone else.
  • Spam, sales, prices, tickets and promoting bars, brands or businesses.
  • Other people's personal data, such as their full name, their address, their phone number or photos of them without their permission.
  • Links, email addresses, phone numbers and @usernames in any text. The title, the description and the nickname go through an automatic filter that rejects most of them and most prices, as well as hate slurs and other banned words.

The chat rules also apply in the route's chat.

6. Drink responsibly

  • Non-alcoholic drinks count: at any stop you can have something without alcohol, and nobody has to drink more than they want.
  • At public events, the route sets at most 2 drinks per stop and there are no speed-drinking challenges: "Speed beer", "Waterfall" and custom rules or punishments can't be used.
  • If you drink, don't drive.
  • In an emergency, call 112.
  • Respect local laws. In some areas of Palma, Llucmajor, Calvià and Sant Antoni de Portmany, public events can't be published: the Balearic decree against excessive tourism bans offering and advertising bar crawls there.

7. Safety tips

  • Always meet in a public place.
  • Tell someone where you're going and who with.
  • You can leave whenever you want, no explanation needed.
  • If you drink, don't drive.
  • In an emergency, call 112.

Your regular routes aren't protected like event routes: don't share the code or your location with strangers. The privacy policy explains why.

8. Reporting and blocking

You can report or block from an event's page (the event or its organiser), from the list of who's going, from the route's participants and, in the chat, by pressing and holding a message.

  • Report: choose a reason (Inappropriate content, Spam or advertising, Dangerous activity, Harassment, Involves minors, Impersonation or Other) and, if you like, tell us what happened in up to 300 characters. We won't say who reported.
  • Block: neither of you will see the other's events or be able to sign up for them, except those already under way or over that you're already going to. If one of you is going to an event of the other's that hasn't started yet, you'll be taken off it. On an event's route you won't see each other's messages. We won't tell them. You can unblock from your profile, under "Blocked people"; if you blocked someone on a route, this phone still won't show you their messages.

If you want us to look into what happened, report it: blocking from an event's page, from a list or from the route's participants doesn't send us a report. In the chat, blocking does.

9. What we do

  • A person reviews every report within 24 hours.
  • If three different people report an event, it's taken off the board and doesn't accept new sign-ups until we've reviewed it. The organiser sees it as "Under review" and can still edit and start it.
  • Removing events: if an event breaks these rules, we remove it, even if it's already under way. The organiser sees the reason and, if there is one, a note from us, and we let everyone who was going or had requested a spot know.
  • Restricting accounts: for a period of time, the account can't organise or join events. Its scheduled events are cancelled, and it's taken off other people's events, including ones already under way. We tell them until when and why.
  • In the chat, we remove messages that break the rules and ban whoever posted them.
  • The organiser can remove someone from their event, including from the route once it's under way.

The text filter, the chat mask, which covers some hate slurs with asterisks when a message is sent or edited, and taking an event off the board after three reports are automatic. Removing events and restricting accounts is always decided by a person.

10. If you disagree

If we've removed your event, taken it off the board while we review some reports, restricted your account, removed one of your chat messages or taken you off a route, or you disagree with what we did about one of your reports, write to us at midwayofdraenor@gmail.com within 6 months of the decision, from your account's email address or telling us your nickname or the name you were using. Tell us which event it is, if there is one, and why you disagree. A person will review it, it won't cost you anything and we'll reply by email. If you disagree with our reply, you can also go to the courts.

We don't tell you what we decide about the reports you make in the app: if you'd like to know, ask us.

You can also tell us by email, even without an account, about an event or content you believe is illegal. Tell us which event it is (for example, its link, or its title, city and day), why you believe it's illegal, your name and email address, and confirm that, as far as you know, what you tell us is accurate and complete. If it's about child sexual abuse, you don't need to identify yourself. We'll confirm we've received it and tell you what we've decided and how to appeal.
