CrawlQuest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

1. Who is responsible

CrawlQuest is a mobile app for organising bar crawls with friends, developed by Jorge Andreu Royo, an independent developer who is the controller of your personal data. You can reach us at midwayofdraenor@gmail.com for any privacy matter.

This policy explains what data the app processes, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and how you can exercise your rights.

2. What data we process

2.1. Account (optional)

You can use CrawlQuest as a guest, without an account. If you sign in with Apple, Google or Facebook, the provider gives us a user identifier, your name and your email address (Apple lets you hide your real email). We never receive or store your password.

2.2. Session data

  • The name you choose to play with and, if you decide to upload one, your profile photo.
  • The session code and settings: stops, active rules and the bars on the route.
  • Your progress in the session: drinks, points and penalties.
  • The messages you write in the session chat.
  • If you have Premium, the rules and punishments you create, which are stored in your account and copied to the sessions where you choose to use them.

2.3. Location

With your permission, and only while the app is open, we use your device's precise location to find nearby bars and build the route and, during an active session, to show your position on the map to the other participants. CrawlQuest does not use your location in the background.

Your latest position is only kept while the session lasts: it is deleted automatically when the session ends and, in any case, 24 hours after its last update. The point where the route was created is also deleted when the session ends.

2.4. Purchases

If you buy a rule pack or CrawlQuest Premium, we store what you bought, when, and the transaction identifier provided by Apple or Google, so that we can unlock it and restore it on your devices. Payment is handled by Apple or Google: we never see your card or payment details.

2.5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store your device's notification identifier to let you know about activity that concerns you. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings.

2.6. Crash diagnostics

When the app fails, a technical report is sent to Sentry: the error, the device model, the system and app versions, and screen performance data. These reports do not include your name, email, messages or location.

2.7. Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's SDK may collect device identifiers, your IP address and ad interaction data to serve ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud. On iPhone, CrawlQuest does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps, so it does not access Apple's advertising identifier. CrawlQuest Premium users do not see ads.

2.8. Preferences

Your language and other app preferences are stored on your own device.

3. Why we use your data and on what legal basis

  • Providing the service (performance of a contract): creating and sharing sessions, building routes, the leaderboard, the chat, and unlocking and restoring your purchases.
  • Location and notifications (consent): requested by the operating system; you can withdraw them at any time in your device settings.
  • Diagnostics and security (legitimate interest): finding and fixing errors and protecting the service against abuse.
  • Advertising (legitimate interest or consent, depending on your region): showing the ads that fund the free version. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Google only uses your data to personalise ads if you have given your consent; otherwise ads are not personalised.
  • Legal obligations: keeping the information required by law, for example about purchases.

4. Who we share it with

4.1. Other participants in your session

They see your name, profile photo, progress and score, your chat messages and, while the session is active, your position on the map.

4.2. Providers that process data on behalf of CrawlQuest

  • Supabase: database, profile photo storage and sign-in. Servers in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Google: Google Maps Platform to find bars and calculate routes, AdMob for advertising, and Sign in with Google.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases and maps on iPhone.
  • Meta: Facebook sign-in, only if you choose it.
  • Sentry: crash reports.
  • Expo: push notification delivery.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Such transfers rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

4.3. We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or hand over your personal data to third parties for their own purposes.

5. How long we keep it

  • Location during a session: until the session ends and, at most, 24 hours after its last update.
  • Unfinished sessions: closed automatically after 6 hours if they never start and after 18 hours if they are in progress.
  • Session history, chat, account and purchases: for as long as you keep your account or until you ask us to delete it, except for anything we must keep by law.
  • Crash reports: for Sentry's retention period, 90 days at most.

6. Your rights

You can request access to your data, its correction or deletion, object to or restrict its processing, and receive it in a portable format. You can also withdraw the location and notification permissions at any time in your device settings.

Deleting your account. If you are signed in, you can delete your account and its data from the app, in Profile → Account → Delete account. The account, recorded purchases, your own rules, statistics and friends are deleted straight away, and your chat messages are withdrawn. If you have Premium, also cancel the subscription in the App Store or Google Play so that it does not renew. If you cannot access the app, ask us by email as described below.

To exercise these rights, or to delete your account and all of its data, write to midwayofdraenor@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account or telling us the name you use in the app. We reply within one month at most. If you disagree with our reply, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or your local supervisory authority.

7. Security

All communication with our servers is encrypted. The database enforces per-user access rules: each person can only read their own data and that of the sessions they take part in. Even so, no system is completely secure.

8. Minors

CrawlQuest is about drinking and is intended only for people aged 18 or over, or the legal drinking age in your country if higher. We do not knowingly collect data from minors; if we find out that we have, we will delete it.

9. Drink responsibly

CrawlQuest is a game for having a good time with friends. Drink in moderation, respect local laws and never drive after drinking.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with its update date.
